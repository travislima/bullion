What a life cover calculator will not tell you

Bullion · Risk cover · Draft

Ninety seconds, one number

Life cover calculators are a genuinely useful starting point. Put in your income, your age and your debt, and you get a figure. The problem is not that the figure is wrong — it is that it is answering a simpler question than the one your family will actually face.

What the calculator does not know

It does not know what cover you already hold through your employer, or whether that cover ends the day you leave. It does not know your bond is in one name and your income in another. It usually does not adjust for inflation, so a figure that supports your family comfortably today buys meaningfully less in fifteen years.

Most importantly, it does not know whether your family would need a lump sum or a monthly income — and those are very different products.
