What happens to your business if a partner dies tomorrow?

Bullion · Business · Draft

The problem nobody plans for

If a co-owner of your business dies tomorrow, their share does not come to you. It goes to their estate, and from there to their heirs — which means you may find yourself in business with a grieving spouse who never wanted to be a shareholder, and who may urgently need cash rather than a shareholding.

Both sides lose in that scenario. The family needs money and holds an asset they cannot easily sell. You need control and cannot afford to buy it.

What a buy-and-sell agreement does

It answers three questions before they become urgent: who buys the share, at what price, and with what money. The first two are legal work. The third is where insurance comes in — policies on each owner's life, structured so the surviving owners have the cash to buy the share at the agreed value.
