Privacy policy

Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA) · Last updated: August 2026

Who we are

Bullion is an authorised Sanlam practice operating in the Eastern Cape, South Africa. This policy explains how we collect, use and protect your personal information when you use this website or contact us.

What we collect

When you submit a form on this site we collect only what you give us: your name, contact details, and the content of your enquiry. We do not collect information in the background, and no form data ever appears in a web address.

Why we collect it

We use your details solely to respond to your enquiry — to call you back, answer a question about a service, or discuss joining our team. We do not sell your information or share it for third-party marketing.

Consent

Every form on this site asks for your explicit consent before you submit it, and records the date and time of that consent. You may withdraw consent at any time by contacting us.

Who we share it with

Where your enquiry relates to a financial product, your information may be shared with Sanlam as our licence holder, strictly for the purpose of serving your request and as required by financial services regulation.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry information only as long as needed to serve you, or as required by financial services record-keeping regulations.

Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask us at any time what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. You may also lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforegulator.org.za.
