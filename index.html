The problem we solve

Great advisers spend too little time advising

Paperwork, compliance, product access and practice management swallow the hours that should go to clients. Bullion MDS carries that weight — so advisers do the work only they can do.

Admin steals advice hours

New business processing, servicing and reporting pile up fast. We run the back office on your behalf, end to end, so your pipeline never stalls on paperwork.

Compliance is a full-time job

Operating under a licensed FSP means fit-and-proper, record-keeping and disclosure duties. Our practice-management framework keeps advisers compliant without the burnout.

Going solo takes years

Building a practice from scratch means product agreements, systems and staff before your first client. Our franchise model gives entrepreneurs a running start.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

What exactly does Bullion MDS do?
We provide insurance products and supporting technology to qualifying financial services providers and financial advisers, together with the back-office administration and practice management that let advisers focus on advising.
I'm an experienced adviser — why partner instead of staying independent?
Most independent advisers lose a large share of their week to processing, servicing and compliance. Partnering with Bullion moves that load onto our team and systems, while you keep the client relationships and the advice.
What does the franchise opportunity involve?
We identify entrepreneurs with the experience, skill set and passion to build and operate a franchise in the financial services industry. You operate the practice; we supply the model, products, technology and business development support behind it.
Where are you based, and who do you serve?
Our office is at On Main Lifestyle Centre on William Moffett Expressway in Gqeberha (Port Elizabeth), Eastern Cape — and we partner with advisers and FSPs across South Africa.
