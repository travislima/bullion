About Bullion MDS

Built in the back office.
Driven by a bigger idea.

We learned the financial services industry from the inside — one policy, one adviser, one practice at a time.

Our story

From administration partner to growth engine

The beginning: back-office specialists

Bullion started as a management and administration services company, rendering back-office administration and financial planner management to planners on behalf of an FSP — so that financial planners could focus on being financial planners.

What the market taught us

Working behind the scenes, we saw the pattern clearly: advisers didn't only need administration. They needed business development resources, broader product distribution, and modern technology — the full machinery of a practice.

Bullion MDS today

Today we provide insurance products and supporting technology to qualifying financial services providers and financial advisers, and we identify entrepreneurs with the experience, skill set and passion to build and operate franchises in the financial services industry — or join our team as advisers.

Where we're going

Our ambition is simple to say and hard to do: multiply the number of excellent financial advisers serving South African families, in every community, until generational wealth stops being the exception.

Financial planning, done correctly, can change a country.

We believe financial planning has the potential to change the socio-economic landscape of South Africa. Insurance done right protects income when it's interrupted, settles estates without destroying them, and passes something on instead of starting over.

Every adviser we support and every franchise we help launch pushes that vision further into the country.

What we stand for

Our values

Advisers first

Our whole model exists so advisers can do their best work. If it doesn't free up advice hours or grow the practice, we don't do it.

Do it properly

Financial services is a trust business. Fit-and-proper standards, clean records and honest disclosure aren't red tape to us — they're the product.

Generational thinking

We measure success in decades, not quarters: practices that outlast their founders and families whose wealth survives them.

Partnership over hierarchy

Franchisees and advisers are partners, not personnel. We win when their practices win — the incentives are built that way on purpose.

Grown in the Eastern Cape

We're proudly built in Gqeberha, and we believe world-class financial services businesses can come from anywhere in South Africa.

Open door

Serious people with serious ambition get a serious conversation. Every enquiry is answered by a person, not a queue.
