Who we are

A complete financial partner

Bullion is an authorised Sanlam practice dedicated to helping individuals and families make confident financial decisions. We provide clear guidance, trusted advice, and lifelong partnership across every stage of your financial journey.

Our practice

Rooted in the Eastern Cape

From our offices in Port Elizabeth (Gqeberha) and Humansdorp, our team of 45 advisors serves individuals, families and business owners across the province — and we're growing to 100 advisors as we expand across the Eastern Cape.

Every client relationship is built the same way: understand your life first, then build the plan around it. Insurance, investments, retirement, health cover and legacy — one partner, one integrated strategy.
