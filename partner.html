Partner with us

Your practice.
Our machinery.

Two pathways into Bullion: join as a financial adviser, or build and operate your own financial services franchise. Both come with the full weight of our products, technology and back office behind you.

Pathway one

Join as a financial adviser

You bring the client relationships and the drive. We bring everything else: FSP-backed products to advise on, technology to work with, and a back office that processes, services and reports so you don't have to.

You'll operate within a practice-management framework that keeps you fit-and-proper, on target and supported — with real people at the end of the phone in Gqeberha.

Apply as an adviser

Who thrives here

Advisers who love client work and are tired of losing their week to processing — from ambitious newcomers to experienced planners ready to scale.

What you get

Product access, advice technology, back-office administration, practice management and business development support.

What we ask

Regulatory fit (fit-and-proper), professionalism with clients, and genuine ambition to grow a practice — not just a book.

Pathway two

Build a Bullion franchise

We identify entrepreneurs with the experience, skill set and — above all — the passion to build and operate a franchise in the financial services industry. You own and run the practice; Bullion supplies the model behind it.

It's the ownership upside of independence without the years of infrastructure-building: products, systems, administration and business development are ready on day one.

Enquire about a franchise

Discovery & fit

We get to know your experience, capital position and ambitions — and you get full transparency on the model.

Launch with the machinery on

Branding, products, technology and back office are live from the start. You focus on clients and your team.

Scale your own team

Recruit and grow advisers under your franchise with our practice-management framework supporting you.
