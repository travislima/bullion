What we do

The machinery behind
a great practice

Six capabilities, one goal: advisers who spend their time advising, and practices that grow because of it.

Back-office administration

New business processing, policy servicing, commission reconciliation and record-keeping — run by specialists on your behalf, so nothing falls through the cracks.

Advice technology

Supporting technology that puts quotes, client records and planning tools in one place — less re-typing, fewer errors, faster turnaround for clients.

Insurance product access

Distribution of FSP-backed insurance products, matched to the realities of South African families — protection that pays out when it matters.

Practice management

Financial planner management done properly: goal setting, production tracking, compliance rhythm and the difficult conversations that keep a practice healthy.

Business development

Dedicated business development resources — lead strategy, market positioning and growth coaching — because a practice that isn't growing is shrinking.

Franchise development

For entrepreneurs: a complete practice-in-a-box — model, products, systems and coaching — to build and operate your own financial services franchise.
