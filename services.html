For business owners

Your business is an asset. Plan for it like one.

Most owners insure the building and the bakkie, then leave the business itself exposed. If a partner dies, or the person who holds the client relationships is off for a year, what happens next is a business question long before it is an insurance one.

Keyperson cover

Cover for the people the business cannot afford to lose, so the company survives the gap while it rebuilds.

Buy-and-sell agreements

Funded agreements so a surviving partner can buy out a share at a fair price — and the family gets paid without a fight.

Business overheads

Cover that keeps rent, salaries and accounts paid while an owner is unable to work.

Employee benefits

Group retirement and risk benefits that help you keep good staff — and give them a plan of their own.
